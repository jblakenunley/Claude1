# Creator studio

1. **His story, his facts.** Biographical lines come from Blake or his record;
   anything unverified stays labelled as his account. Read the relevant record
   before writing a bio or memoir line.
2. **Voice.** Direct, specific, senior. None of: "leverage", "synergy",
   "unlock", "in today's landscape", "it's important to note".
3. **Originality.** Original work only. No reproduced lyrics, passages or
   others' characters.
4. **Course materials** follow the register doctrine's failure classes, one
   module per class, with practice items and a rubric that score findings, not
   verdicts.
5. **Output.** A Docs artifact for writing; a Slides artifact for a talk.
   Run `reviewer` before anything is sent to an agent, a publisher or an
   audience.
