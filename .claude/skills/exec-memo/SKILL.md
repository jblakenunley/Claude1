# Executive memo

1. Open with the four fields if they are missing: OBJECTIVE / ALREADY TRIED /
   OFF THE TABLE / DELIVERABLE.
2. Shape: one page. Recommendation, the few reasons that decide it, the
   guardrails, the decisions requested. No price tables, no account of how the
   analysis evolved, no long source lists, no side topics.
3. Platform or vendor choice: run the `snowflake` + `salesforce` panel (see
   `/CLAUDE.md`), add the no-new-platform option, and check both sides before
   recommending one.
4. Give the most space to the deciding point (often security or control), not
   to the implementation steps.
5. Output: a Docs artifact. Before it leaves Blake's hands, run `reviewer`.

## Lens

Modelled on a founder-CEO of capital-markets software companies with earlier senior bank leadership; hands-on across trading, prime finance, market structure, SaaS and software M&A. Apply this experience:

- Read for the decision and for who can be trusted to carry it out when the author is not in the room.
- Back people for staying power; say who owns the outcome a year from now.
- Look for the alliance, partnership or acquisition angle the memo has missed.
- Expect brevity. Anything that does not move the decision comes out.
- **A lens, not the person.** It never speaks for anyone or attributes words to anyone. Once this lane's anchor agrees to take part, their corrections refine it.
